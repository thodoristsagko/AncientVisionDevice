# AncientVision — How to Use (Paros 2026)

## What You Have

You have two things:

1. **A small orange device** (the sensor) — this feels vibrations in the ground
2. **A phone** (with the app already installed) — this shows you if the ground is safe

The sensor talks to the phone using Bluetooth (like wireless headphones).

---

## Step 1: Charge the Sensor

Plug the USB-C cable into the sensor (same cable type as most new phones). Plug the other end into any charger or power bank.

- Charging takes about **1 hour**
- A full charge lasts **4-6 hours**
- You'll see a battery icon on the sensor's tiny screen while it charges

---

## Step 2: Turn the Sensor On

Press the **button on the left side** of the sensor. The small screen lights up and shows "AncientVision". That's it — it's on and working.

To turn it off later, **hold that same button for 6 seconds**.

---

## Step 3: Put the Sensor on the Ground

Place the sensor **directly on the ground** next to the excavation trench. Screen facing up.

**Important:**
- It must touch the ground — not sit on a table, chair, or bag
- Put it at the edge of the trench, near where people are digging
- Don't put it where people are walking (footsteps confuse it)

---

## Step 4: Connect the Phone to the Sensor

1. Open the **AncientVision** app on the phone
2. Tap the **shield icon** at the bottom right — this is the Safety screen
3. Tap **Scan**
4. Wait a few seconds — a device called **"AncientVision"** will appear
5. Tap it — the screen will turn **green** and say **Connected**

If it doesn't connect, make sure:
- Bluetooth is turned on (check phone settings)
- The phone is within **10 meters** of the sensor
- The sensor is turned on (screen is lit)

---

## Step 5: Understanding the Colors

The app shows a **big colored bar**. This is the only thing you need to watch:

| Color | What It Means | What To Do |
|-------|--------------|------------|
| **GREEN** | Everything is fine | Keep working normally |
| **YELLOW** | Something small detected | Pay attention, keep working carefully |
| **ORANGE** | Something concerning | Stop digging, look around, check the trench walls |
| **RED** | DANGER | **STOP EVERYTHING. LEAVE THE TRENCH NOW.** |

---

## Step 6: When the Phone Alerts You

If the ground vibrations are dangerous, the phone will:
- **Vibrate** strongly
- **Play a loud alarm**
- **Show a big warning** on the whole screen

**Read what it says**, then tap to close the warning.

To turn off the alarm sound, tap the **speaker icon**. The phone will still vibrate and show visual warnings.

---

## Step 7: First Day Setup (Do This Once)

The first time you use the system at a new location, let it "learn" what normal ground vibrations feel like:

1. Connect everything (steps 1-4)
2. Tap the **small graph icon** on the safety screen to see more details
3. Tap the **tuning icon** (top right, looks like a dial)
4. Tap **Start Site Learning**
5. **Don't dig** for about 10 minutes — let the site be quiet
6. After 10 minutes, the system knows what "normal" feels like for this spot

You only do this **once per trench location**.

---

## Step 8: If Something Goes Wrong

| What Happened | What To Do |
|--------------|------------|
| Sensor screen is black | Press the side button. If nothing happens, charge it |
| Phone says "Disconnected" | Tap **Reconnect**. Move the phone closer to the sensor |
| Phone can't find the sensor | Turn Bluetooth on in phone settings. Turn sensor off and on again |
| Alarms keep going off for no reason | The sensor is probably near footsteps or a road. Move it to quieter ground |
| App is frozen | Close the app completely and reopen it. Your data is saved |

---

## Step 9: Other Things the App Can Do

You don't need these for safety, but they're useful:

- **Quick Capture**: Take a photo of something you found + add a quick note
- **Manual Entry**: Write a full description of an artifact
- **Field Journal**: Write daily notes about what happened at the site
- **Coin AI**: Take a photo of a coin and the AI tries to identify it
- **Analytics**: See charts of everything you've recorded
- **Export**: Save all your data as files (CSV, JSON, etc.)

Find these in the **Tools** tab (wrench icon at the bottom).

---

## SAFETY RULES

1. **This device helps you, but it does NOT replace proper safety practices**
2. Always shore up trench walls according to regulations
3. If the phone shows **RED** — leave the trench. Don't wait. Don't "check first". Leave.
4. Tell the site supervisor about **every** yellow, orange, or red alert
5. If the sensor or phone stops working, **do not** assume the ground is safe
